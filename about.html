Our Story

A family salon, a community fixture, a 25-year legacy — and a fresh new chapter.

Who We Are

More than a salon.
A Vauxhall institution.

London Look Hair & Beauty began with one woman's passion — a vision to create not just a place to get your hair done, but a warm, welcoming space that would become part of the community fabric of Vauxhall.

For over 25 years, that vision has held true. Originally founded and rooted in London's Portuguese community, the salon has always been about more than haircuts. It's about belonging. It's about people. It's about walking in as a stranger and leaving as a regular.

Today, the salon is carried forward with pride by the founder's daughter — honouring everything that made this place special while bringing fresh energy, modern techniques and a new generation of loyal clients through the door.
